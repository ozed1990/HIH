Home Improvement Hub helps U.S. homeowners finish the projects they've been putting off, with calm, clear, step-by-step guides you can follow on your phone while you work.

What you'll find here

Our guides are organized by room and project: Kitchen, Bathroom, Flooring, Painting & Walls, Plumbing, Electrical, Doors & Windows, Outdoor & Garden, Storage & Organization and Home Maintenance. Every guide tells you up front how hard the job is, how long it takes, roughly what it costs, and exactly which tools and materials to have ready.

How we write our guides

  • One action per step. Each step starts with what to do and ends with a "Done right" check, so you know you got it right before moving on. You can mark steps done as you go, and your progress is saved on your device.
  • Checked against real sources. We work from manufacturer instructions, building-code basics and official sources such as ENERGY STAR, and we use exact sizes and quantities instead of vague advice.
  • Pictures that explain. Our diagrams are drawn for each guide to show what words can't: a measurement, an angle, a part. Videos are chosen from trusted creators, and we watch them before adding them.
  • Safety first. Every guide flags the hazards and tells you when to stop and call a licensed pro. We only write how-tos for jobs a homeowner can do legally and safely. For work like electrical panels, gas lines or anything on the roof, we explain what to expect when you hire a professional instead.
  • Kept current. Prices, rules and incentives change, so we update guides when they do.

A note on our advice

Our guides are general information for typical homes. Your house, local codes and permit rules may be different, so check them before you start, follow your product's instructions, and hire a licensed professional whenever a job is beyond your comfort or skill level.
