This Privacy Policy explains what information Home Improvement Hub ("we", "us") collects when you visit homeimprovementhub101.com (the "Site"), how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. Your email address when you sign up for our newsletter, and whatever you include when you email us.
  • Information collected automatically. Details about your device and visit, such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, the site that referred you, and approximate location. This is collected through cookies, pixels and similar technologies, described below.
  • Information that stays on your device. When you tick off tools in a checklist or mark a step done in a guide, your browser saves that progress in its own local storage. It is not sent to us, and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

How we use information

  • To run, maintain and improve the Site and our guides.
  • To send the newsletter you signed up for. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To answer your messages.
  • To understand which guides are read and useful (analytics).
  • To show ads on the Site and measure our own ads on other platforms.
  • To keep the Site secure, prevent fraud and comply with the law.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

We and our partners use cookies and similar technologies. The main services on the Site are:

  • Shopify hosts the Site and stores newsletter sign-ups. See Shopify's privacy policy.
  • Google Analytics helps us understand how visitors use the Site. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Meta Pixel and TikTok Pixel measure our ads on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok and may be used to show you our ads on those platforms based on your visits here. See Meta's privacy policy and TikTok's privacy policy.
  • Google AdSense shows ads on the Site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this Site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the internet. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your ad choices: you can opt out of personalized advertising from Google in Google Ads Settings, and from many other third-party vendors at aboutads.info/choices. You can manage ads on Meta in your Meta ad preferences and on TikTok in the app's ads settings. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some parts of the Site may not work as well without them.

Affiliate links

Some links on the Site are affiliate links to partner companies, often through an affiliate network. When you click one, the partner or network may set a cookie to record that you came from us, so we can earn a commission if you buy or request a quote. If you fill out a form or make a purchase on a partner's website, that partner's privacy policy applies to the information you give them, not this one.

Embedded videos

Guide videos are hosted on YouTube. A video only loads when you press play, and it uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. Once you play a video, Google and YouTube may collect information as described in Google's privacy policy.

How we share information

  • With service providers that run the Site and the newsletter for us, such as Shopify.
  • With the analytics and advertising partners listed above, through their cookies and pixels.
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, our visitors or the public.
  • As part of a merger, sale or transfer of the Site, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

We do not sell your name or email address. Under some U.S. state laws, letting ad partners collect information through cookies and pixels for targeted advertising can count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information. You can opt out as described below.

Your privacy choices and rights

  • Newsletter. Unsubscribe at any time with the link in any email.
  • Cookies and ads. Use your browser settings and the opt-out links above.
  • U.S. state privacy rights. Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and other states with privacy laws), you may have the right to know what personal information we have about you, get a copy of it, correct it, delete it, and opt out of its sale or sharing and of targeted advertising. Where required by law, we treat a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal from your browser as a request to opt out. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.
  • EU and UK visitors. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your personal information, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. We rely on your consent for non-essential cookies and the newsletter, on our legitimate interests to run and secure the Site, and on legal obligations where they apply. Your information may be processed in the United States and other countries. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

To opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising, use our Your Privacy Choices page. For any other request, email us at contact@homeimprovementhub101.com with "Privacy request" in the subject line. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it, and we'll respond within the time the law requires.

Children

The Site is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep information

We keep your newsletter email until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, and emails you send us for as long as needed to answer them. Analytics and advertising partners keep data according to their own policies and settings.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, and the Site is served over HTTPS. No website or online service is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Site or the law changes. We'll change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we may also post a notice on the Site.
