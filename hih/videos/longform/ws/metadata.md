**Title:** Water Softener Maintenance: 6 Easy Jobs That Keep It Working

**Description:**
Hard water coming back? Spotty glasses, crusty faucets and soap that won't lather usually mean your water softener needs a little attention. This animated cutaway shows how a softener works inside, then walks through the 6 maintenance jobs that keep it working: checking the salt, using the right salt, breaking salt bridges, cleaning the brine tank, cleaning the resin and clearing the injector (venturi).
